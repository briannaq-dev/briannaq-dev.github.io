Spotify Song Graph Recommender

Graph database

Spring 2026 DS4300

A music recommendation engine built as a graph database. I modeled songs as nodes in Neo4j and connected each one to its 10 nearest neighbors by Euclidean distance over nine standardized audio features, producing a graph of 1,046 songs and 10,460 similarity relationships. I then wrote the Cypher queries that start from songs by two seed artists and recommend similar songs by other artists, filtered by genre and ranked by average distance.

TA Assignment Optimizer

Evolutionary algorithm

Fall 2024 DS3500

A multi-objective evolutionary algorithm, written from scratch in Python, that assigns 43 TAs to 17 lab sections while balancing five competing objectives: overallocation, time conflicts, undersupport, unwilling assignments, and unpreferred assignments. It maintains a Pareto front of non-dominated solutions and picks a best solution with a weighted score. I also added a decorator-based profiler and unit tests for every objective function.

Data engineering
