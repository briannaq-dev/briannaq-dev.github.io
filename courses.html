Coursework Highlights

My coursework pairs a data science and computer science core (machine learning, databases, software development, mobile apps, and data visualization) with business and finance (corporate finance, investments, accounting, statistics, marketing, and fintech).

By semester

Fall 2026

In progress 4 courses
  • DS 4420 Advanced Machine Learning
    Continues supervised and unsupervised learning with a focus on the math behind the algorithms: kernel methods, models for sequences and time series, Bayesian parameter estimation, generative models, and sampling methods such as MCMC.
  • FINA 2730 Fintech and Financial Innovation
    The history and drivers of fintech and financial innovation, payments and platform economics, and how new technology challenges traditional financial institutions.
  • FINA 4335 Computational Methods and Applications in Finance
    Python as a financial data analytics tool, starting with NumPy, Pandas, and statistics libraries and moving to applications in finance.
  • MKTG 4604 Creating Business Value with Data and AI Technologies
    How firms generate and capture value from data and AI in the digital economy.

4 courses

Advanced Placement

6 courses
  • AP Computer Science Principles
    The central ideas of computer science and computational thinking, and how computing changes the world, with an emphasis on creative problem solving.
  • AP Macroeconomics
    Economic systems as a whole: national income and price levels, economic performance measures, the financial sector, stabilization policy, growth, and international economics.
  • AP Microeconomics
    The principles behind individual economic decisions, how product and factor markets work, income distribution, market failure, and the role of government, analyzed with graphs, charts, and data.

    The scientific study of behavior and mental processes, covering the major subfields of psychology, its research methods and ethics, and the biological, behavioral, cognitive, humanistic, psychodynamic, and sociocultural perspectives.
  • AP Statistics
    The major concepts and tools for collecting, analyzing, and drawing conclusions from data: exploring data, planning studies, probability and simulation, and statistical inference.
